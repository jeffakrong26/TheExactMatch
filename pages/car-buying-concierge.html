What does a car buying concierge actually do? +
Searches our dealer network for exactly what you asked for, sends you 3 real matched options within 24 hours, and — if you want it — negotiates the price and coordinates the closing on your behalf. The search and matching are free; full-service negotiation is the optional White Glove tier.
Is this only for Texas? +
No — the dealer network is nationwide. Texas is where the business is based and where reach runs deepest, but the same search and matching process works for buyers anywhere in the US.

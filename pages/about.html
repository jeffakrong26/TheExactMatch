About

He used to sell for the dealership.
Now he works for you.

Jeff Akrong spent years on the dealership side of the negotiation — brand-specializing across Audi, Porsche, Aston Martin, Rolls-Royce and Bentley, then on the Mercedes-Benz side at Swickard Automotive. That's the exact playbook dealers use against buyers every day. The Exact Match points it the other way — entirely on the buyer's side, for free.

Before any of that, Jeff founded Exclusive Auto Services, managing the fleet for an exotic car rental company — including facilitating fleet vehicle sales and purchases — and working as an online buyer for private-party clients hunting hard-to-find exotics. That's the direct model behind The Exact Match today: sourcing a specific car for a specific person, not selling from a lot.

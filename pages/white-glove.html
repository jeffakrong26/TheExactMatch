Which tier am I?

The tier is set by how specific your brief is, not by what the car costs. A tight spec — exact color, exact interior, exact options — is Hard to Find, even if it's a Mercedes. A loose brief with room to work is Regular. Specialty 911s (GT, GT3, classics, or a tight spec) are Hard to Find; an older general 911 or a loose brief is Regular.

What the fee covers

Finding your car is always free. Running the actual deal — negotiating, coordinating inspections, arranging transport — is real, hands-on work on your behalf, and that's what the White Glove fee covers.

The fine print, up front

  • Deposit counts toward the fee. Balance due at signing, when the deal is agreed — not at delivery. The fee is earned at agreement.
  • No refund after the final payment.
  • If we can't find a car that meets your brief in budget: regular searches get a market update and a rethink together at day 30; if we hear nothing by day 45, we keep the deposit. Hard to Find searches get a check-in every 30 days until the car is found, no hard deadline. A refund is discussed case by case — never automatic.
  • Deposit is also kept if you walk away, ghost, or turn down a car that meets your brief.

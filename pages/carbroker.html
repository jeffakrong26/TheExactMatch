Car Broker

An auto broker who works for the buyer, not the lot.

A traditional dealership only shows you what's already on its lot. As your broker, we go find the exact car you want across our nationwide dealer network, negotiate the price, and — if you want — handle the whole purchase for you.

What a broker does here

  • Searches dealer inventory nationwide against your exact spec — including cars not yet listed publicly
  • Sends you 3 real matched options within 24 hours, free, always
  • Negotiates the out-the-door price on your behalf, if you want the full deal handled
  • Coordinates paperwork and delivery so you're not the one chasing a dealership's finance office

Free search, optional full service

Search and matching are free — always. Full negotiation and paperwork handling is the separate, optional White Glove tier: a flat fee for standard vehicles, a custom quote (capped at $7,000) for hard-to-find ones.

Who's behind it

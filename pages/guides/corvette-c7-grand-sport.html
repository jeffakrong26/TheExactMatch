The 2017–2019 Corvette Grand Sport: A Real Buyer's Guide.

The C7 Grand Sport puts the Stingray's 460-hp V8 in the Z06's wide body, brakes, cooling and suspension. It cost about $66,000 new, and clean examples still trade in the mid-$50,000s to mid-$70,000s. The new C8 Grand Sport starts at $88,495. Below is what matters before you buy one: the specs, current market pricing, the known issues (wheels especially), running costs, and what to check on a specific car.

Specs At A Glance

The numbers that matter.

ENGINE 6.2L LT1 V8, dry sump POWER 460 hp TORQUE 465 lb-ft 0–60 3.6s (Z07, auto) BUILT 26,280
  • Transmission: 7-speed manual with Active Rev Match, or 8-speed paddle-shift automatic (8L90). Coupe (with removable roof panel) or convertible.
  • Chassis: Z06-derived wide body, cooling and suspension tuning. Magnetic Ride Control and an electronic limited-slip differential are standard.
  • Brakes and tires: Brembo 6-piston front and 4-piston rear. Michelin Pilot Super Sport run-flats, 285/30ZR19 front and 335/25ZR20 rear.
  • Z07 package: carbon-ceramic brakes and Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 tires. 2,028 Grand Sports got it.
  • Production: 21,977 coupes and 4,303 convertibles across 2017–2019. Only 5,546 (about 21%) were manuals.

0–60 is Chevrolet's figure for a Z07 car with the 8-speed automatic; MotorWeek tested 3.9s. Production figures as reported by CorvetteBlogger from GM data.

Why It Makes Sense Right Now

Z06 hardware for Stingray money.

The Grand Sport was the sweet spot of the C7 range. It has the Z06's chassis, brakes and cooling without the supercharger's heat and running costs. A 2019 Grand Sport cost $66,590 new, about $11,000 more than a Stingray.

Seven to nine years later, low-mileage cars are listed at or near their original base price, and below it with more miles. For comparison, Classic.com's C7 Z06 benchmark is around $76,000. The new 2027 C8 Grand Sport starts at $88,495 for a 1LT coupe and $94,595 for a 2LT. That one is mid-engine, with 535 hp, and it's a different kind of car. If you want a front-engine V8 Corvette with a manual option and the wide-body hardware, the C7 Grand Sport is the last one.

Current Market Pricing

What the Grand Sport is actually selling for.

Based on 66 Grand Sports for sale market-wide in September–October 2026, Classic.com lists an average recorded sale price of about $65,110. Mileage matters most. Cars under about 15,000 miles are listed from the low $60,000s to the mid-$70,000s. Cars with 20,000–45,000 miles mostly sit in the mid-$50,000s, with 2019s and 3LTs pulling higher.

Is It Reliable?

The honest answer.

The LT1 V8 is a proven pushrod engine and is not the weak point. Two issues are well documented on these cars:

  • Bent and cracked wheels. This is the big one. The Grand Sport and Z06 share wide cast wheels that owners say bend and crack under normal driving. Car and Driver's 40,000-mile long-term Grand Sport needed seven wheel repairs and three replacements, $4,098 in total. Several class actions followed, and GM set up a special reimbursement program for wheel damage during the warranty and extended coverage period. The catch is that the owner has to show the damage wasn't caused by a road hazard.
  • 8-speed automatic shudder. The 8L90 can develop a torque-converter shudder. GM's bulletin (TSB 18-NA-355, covering 2015–2019 Corvettes) calls for flushing the transmission with updated Mobil 1 LV ATF HP fluid, and that usually fixes it. Early cars are the most affected.

Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and have a Chevrolet dealer check for open campaigns and bulletins.

Ownership Costs

What to budget for.

  • Oil changes: the dry-sump system holds about 10 quarts of dexos2-approved oil (GM specifies Mobil 1 0W-40), so changes cost more than on a typical V8. The oil life monitor sets the interval.
  • Automatic transmission: if the fluid-flush bulletin hasn't been done, budget for it.
  • Tires: the factory run-flats have been listed at roughly $460–$650 per tire depending on size, so a full set runs about $2,000 or more installed. Z07 cars on Cup 2s wear through tires faster.
  • Wheels: on a car that sees rough roads, set money aside for wheel straightening or replacement.
  • Z07 carbon-ceramic brakes: they last a long time on the street, but replacement rotors are very expensive. Check rotor condition before you buy.

Before You Buy

What to look for.

  • Every wheel, inside and out. Have each wheel checked on a balancer for bends and look at the inner barrels for cracks. Ask for receipts for any wheel repairs.
  • Transmission history. On automatics, look for the fluid-flush bulletin in the service records. On manuals, check clutch engagement and ask about track use.
  • Track use. Look at brake pad and rotor wear, tire wear patterns, and any aftermarket brake fluid or pads. These cars are built for the track, and plenty have been on one.
  • Z07 cars. Check the carbon-ceramic rotors for wear and chipping. Replacing them is the single biggest cost on the car.
  • Roof and top. On coupes, check the removable roof panel's seals and latches for leaks and rattles. On convertibles, run the top through a full cycle.
  • Special editions. The 2017 Collector Edition (3LT only, Watkins Glen Gray with a Tension Blue interior, about 850 for the US) and the Heritage package are documented on the window sticker. Confirm with the RPO codes on the car's build sheet.
Questions

Straight answers.

How much is a used C7 Corvette Grand Sport?

As of fall 2026, dealer asking prices run from the mid-$50,000s for cars with 20,000–45,000 miles to the mid-$70,000s for low-mile 2019s. Classic.com's average recorded sale is about $65,110.

Do C7 Grand Sport wheels crack?

It's the best-documented issue on the car. The Grand Sport and Z06 share wide cast wheels that owners report bending and cracking, and the problem led to class actions and a GM reimbursement program for damage during warranty coverage. Have every wheel checked on a balancer before you buy.

Manual or automatic?

Only about 21% of Grand Sports were built as manuals, so a manual car takes more searching. The 8-speed automatic is quicker on paper, but check that it has had GM's fluid-flush bulletin for torque-converter shudder.

C7 Grand Sport or the new C8 Grand Sport?

They're different cars. The 2027 C8 Grand Sport is mid-engine with 535 hp and starts at $88,495. The C7 is the last front-engine Grand Sport, offers a manual, and costs well under that on the used market.

The Dealer Email Scripts

Exactly what to send, and what to say when they don't reply by email.

Script 1The first email

Send to the internet sales manager at 3–5 dealers that have your exact car.

Subject: OTD quote request — [Year Make Model Trim], stock #[number] Hi [Name], I'm ready to buy a [Year Make Model Trim] in [color] this week. I'm interested in stock #[number] / VIN [last 8]. I'm requesting out-the-door quotes from several dealers and will buy from whoever gives me the best itemized number. Please send: – Vehicle selling price – Any dealer-installed add-ons (and whether they're removable) – Doc fee – Tax, title, and registration – All manufacturer rebates applied I have my own financing arranged and I'm not trading anything in for this quote. I'm not able to come in until the number is settled, so please reply by email. Thanks, [Your name]

Script 2The follow-up (once you have competing quotes)

Take the best written quote back to the others — once.

Hi [Name], Thanks for the quote. I have a written out-the-door offer of $[amount] on the same car from another dealer. If you can beat it, I'll sign with you this week — otherwise I'll go with them. What's the best you can do? [Your name]

When they call instead of emailing

Keep it short. Say exactly this:

  • "I appreciate the call. I'm only working with written out-the-door numbers right now — can you email it over?"
  • If they ask what payment you want: "I'm not shopping by payment. What's the out-the-door price?"
  • If they say the price is only good today: that's your answer about whether it's a real number.

Walking away

If the number isn't where it needs to be, say so plainly, thank them, and leave — calmly, not as a bluff. It's the single most effective move in the whole process. A call back within a day or two with a better number is common.

The OTD checklist

Any quote you accept must itemize, in writing:

  • Vehicle selling price
  • Every dealer add-on, listed separately (nitrogen, VIN etching, protection packages, market adjustments)
  • Doc fee
  • Tax, title, registration
  • All rebates applied — and whether they were already folded into the "discount"
  • Trade-in value handled as a separate negotiation, after the purchase price is set
